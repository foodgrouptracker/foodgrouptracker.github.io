Food Group Tracker: Configure Plan

Set a client's plan, then send them the link or show them the QR code. Nothing is saved anywhere; the whole plan travels inside the link.

Standard plan

The everyday plan. One box = one serving from the food lists. Calories are estimated from the boxes; you can type over the estimate. Set a row to 0 to leave that food group out of the client's tracker entirely.

Calorie estimate assumes Milk: Protein: Starch 80 · Fruit 60 · Vegetable 25 · Fat 45

Variants

Optional. A different set of counts for certain days, e.g. "Active day". Boxes above the standard plan appear dashed. With a schedule, the app selects the variant on those days; the client can still switch.

Logging

By default the client can check a box by tapping it or by logging a food; every tap still appears in their day's log. Turn this on for a client you want keeping a food record.

Notes for the client

Anything that isn't a count. Shown on their Plan screen.

Link

Text link

The client opens the link once. The app reads the plan and keeps it on their phone.

What the client sees

Left: their Today screen before any boxes are checked. Right: their Plan screen. Tap a variant name on the left to preview that day.
